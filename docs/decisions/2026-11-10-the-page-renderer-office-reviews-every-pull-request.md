## The office that renders cards as pages reviews every pull request (10 Nov 2026)

**Chosen:** every pull request in this repository is reviewed by the office that maintains the
page renderer, because the card format is shared between the Slack and page renderers and a
change to it affects both. The maintainer office still merges once checks are green and that
review is in, as the decision of 9 Nov 2026 says.

**Not chosen:**
- Any reviewer will do: a format change could land without the other renderer's owner seeing it.

**Evidence:** decided by the house's project-management office under Jeremy's delegation of 10 Nov 2026
