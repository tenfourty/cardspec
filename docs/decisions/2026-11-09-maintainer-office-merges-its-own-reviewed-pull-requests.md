## This repository's maintainer office merges its own green, reviewed pull requests (9 Nov 2026)

**Chosen:** the office that maintains this repository merges its own pull requests once their
checks are green and they have been reviewed. It merges locally as an ordinary merge commit, the
same flow it uses for its other repository. This keeps one way of landing changes across both
repositories and avoids a hand-off for every change.

**Not chosen:**
- The homelab maintainer lands the pull requests: adds a hand-off to another office for every change.
- The owner merges each pull request personally: slows every change and makes one person the bottleneck.

**Evidence:** confirmed (Jeremy, 9 Nov 2026)
