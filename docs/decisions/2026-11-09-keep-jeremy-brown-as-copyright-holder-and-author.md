## Jeremy Brown stays the copyright holder and author of the card library (9 Nov 2026)

**Chosen:** keep "Jeremy Brown" as the copyright holder and author. The library will be published
under his personal GitHub organisation and on PyPI, with json-render's Apache-2.0 notice beside
it. This is easy to change before the first release and hard to change after it.

**Not chosen:**
- The organisation name (tenfourty): not preferred for a library published under a personal organisation.
- "The cardspec authors": a collective name, not preferred over naming the author.

**Evidence:** confirmed (Jeremy, 9 Nov 2026)
